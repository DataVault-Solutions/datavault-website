Service 01

Wholesale / Distribution Analytics-as-a-Service

A reliable, ongoing analytics capability for businesses whose operational data is spread across systems, exports and spreadsheets.

Who this is for

Wholesale, distribution and operations-led SMEs that need clear management information across sales, margin, inventory, customers and suppliers—without relying on fragile manual reporting.

Common problems

  • Management reports assembled manually each week or month.
  • ERP, Excel, accounting and operational systems that do not reconcile easily.
  • Limited visibility of stock risk, customer performance or profitability.
  • KPIs that vary by report or stakeholder.

What we build

The service creates an analytics layer between source systems and decision-makers: automated data ingestion, reliable data models, Power BI semantic models and reporting that supports specific operational conversations.

Operational sourcesERP · Excel · supplier files
Data engineeringIngestion · checks · transformation
Trusted modelFacts · dimensions · definitions
Decision layerPower BI · scheduled reporting

How the service works

We start with discovery and an assessment of data, reporting needs and constraints. The initial delivery establishes the foundation; the ongoing service prioritises agreed improvements, new analysis and quality reviews in a structured rhythm.

What ongoing analytics includes

Monthly KPI and data-quality reviews, prioritised dashboard improvements and stakeholder requests. Quarterly model optimisation, architecture review and roadmap planning. The scope is planned jointly and does not imply unlimited work.
