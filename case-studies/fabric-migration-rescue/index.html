Migration and validation

The representative plan sequences workload discovery, target design, proof of concept where needed, controlled migration, reconciliation against agreed source figures and release documentation. It prioritises reliability and traceability over a wholesale technical replacement.

Governance and performance

Workspace structure, access, deployment approach, ownership, documentation and lifecycle controls are designed alongside the technical work. Performance is measured against the actual environment; this case study deliberately does not invent a before/after benchmark.
